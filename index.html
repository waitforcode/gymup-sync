Keep your workout
history locally.

GymUp Sync downloads your GymUp backups from Google Drive and imports your workout history on your own computer.

How it works

  1. GymUp creates backups in your Google Drive account.
  2. You authorize a locally installed rclone client to read the backup folder.
  3. The sync tool downloads and validates a backup, then imports workouts, exercises and sets into local files. It can run on a schedule on your computer.

About the Google permission

The requested drive.readonly permission allows reading files across your Google Drive. The sync tool is configured to use the GymUp backup folder. It downloads files and does not upload, edit or delete files in Drive.

Where your data goes

Downloaded backups and imported workout records are stored on your computer. This information website does not receive workout files or Google credentials.

This integration is operated by its account owner for personal use. It is independent of the developers of GymUp, Google Drive and rclone.
