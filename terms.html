Purpose

GymUp Sync is a personal desktop integration that downloads GymUp backups from an authorized Google Drive account and imports workout records locally. This website describes that integration; it does not offer a hosted synchronization account.

Authorization and local operation

Authorize only an account and files you are entitled to access. The account owner controls the local configuration, schedule, downloaded files and credentials, and can stop synchronization or revoke authorization at any time.

Data handling is described in the privacy policy.

Availability and accuracy

Synchronization depends on the local computer, internet connectivity, available backups and Google API access. Imports may be delayed or fail because of quotas, expired authorization, incompatible backup formats or incomplete files.

Review import reports when relying on the results and retain the original backups. The integration does not guarantee uninterrupted synchronization or complete source data.

Third-party products

GymUp, Google Drive and rclone are separate products governed by their own terms and licenses. This personal integration is not an official product of their developers.

Contact

Questions about this integration can be directed to the developer support email shown on its Google authorization screen.
